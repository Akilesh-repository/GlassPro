Our Journey

Our History

A straightforward look at our steady growth from a single garage bay to the region's most trusted auto glass specialists.

2011

The Beginning

GlassPro opened its doors with a single bay garage, focusing strictly on local windshield repair with a commitment to quality over volume.

2015

Tinting Division

Expanded our services to include premium ceramic and carbon window tinting, building out specialized clean-rooms in a new facility.

2018

Mobile Expansion

Launched our fleet of fully equipped mobile repair vans, bringing our expert services directly to our clients' homes and workplaces.

2026

Regional Leaders

Today, we are the #1 rated auto glass shop in the state, servicing thousands of vehicles a year with an unyielding dedication to safety.
