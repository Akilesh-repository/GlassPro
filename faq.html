Window Tinting Services

We strongly recommend keeping your windows rolled up for 3 to 5 days after tint installation. This allows the film adhesive to fully cure to the glass. Rolling them down too early can cause the tint to catch and peel.
Carbon tint provides excellent UV protection and a sleek matte finish that won't fade. Ceramic tint utilizes advanced nano-ceramic technology to block significantly more infrared (IR) heat, keeping your interior much cooler while ensuring zero cell signal interference.
While we offer extensive mobile services for auto glass repair and replacement, window tinting requires a strictly controlled, dust-free environment to ensure a flawless, bubble-free application. Therefore, all tinting must be completed at our facility.
High-quality films are designed to reduce daytime glare without significantly impairing nighttime visibility. However, very dark shades (low VLT percentage) can reduce visibility in unlit areas. We will help you select a safe and legal shade for your needs.
Wait at least a week before cleaning your newly tinted windows. When you do, use a soft microfiber towel and an ammonia-free glass cleaner. Ammonia will degrade the tint over time and cause it to discolor and peel.
Tint turns purple and bubbles when cheap, dye-based films degrade under harsh UV rays and extreme heat. At GlassPro, we use only premium carbon and ceramic films that are guaranteed not to fade, bubble, or turn purple.
